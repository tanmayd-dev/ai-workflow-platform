LOAN APPROVAL POLICY v2.0

1. AUTOMATED APPROVAL CRITERIA
   - Credit Score must be >= 750
   - Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio must be < 30%
   - Loan Amount must be <= $50,000
   - Employment status: Employed for > 2 years

2. MANUAL REVIEW REQUIRED
   - Credit Score 650-749
   - DTI Ratio 30-45%
   - Self-employed applicants
   - Loan Amounts > $50,000

3. AUTOMATIC REJECTION
   - Credit Score < 650
   - DTI Ratio > 50%
   - Recent bankruptcy (< 7 years)

4. COMPLIANCE CHECKS
   - CIP (Customer Identification Program) verification required
   - OFAC check must be CLEAR
   - TILA (Truth in Lending Act) disclosure generated
