FRAUD DETECTION POLICY - RETAIL BANKING

Document Version: 1.2
Effective Date: January 2026
Department: Risk Management

POLICY OVERVIEW
This document establishes the fraud detection and prevention policy for retail banking transactions. All transactions must be evaluated against defined risk criteria before approval.

RISK THRESHOLDS
1. Transaction Amount Limits
   - Low Risk: Transactions under $1,000
   - Medium Risk: Transactions between $1,000 and $10,000
   - High Risk: Transactions over $10,000

2. Geographic Risk Assessment
   - Domestic transactions: Standard review
   - International transactions: Enhanced review required
   - High-risk countries: Mandatory secondary approval

3. Customer Behavior Analysis
   - Monitor for unusual patterns
   - Flag transactions inconsistent with customer history
   - Require additional verification for suspicious activity

DECISION CRITERIA
- Automatic Approval: Low risk score AND domestic AND under $1,000
- Manual Review Required: Medium risk score OR international
- Immediate Hold: High risk score OR blacklisted country OR fraud indicators

COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
All decisions must be logged with complete audit trail including:
- Transaction details
- Risk assessment results
- Decision rationale
- Approver information (if manual review)
- Timestamp of all actions

ESCALATION PROCEDURES
Suspicious transactions must be escalated to:
1. First Level: Branch Manager (Medium Risk)
2. Second Level: Regional Risk Officer (High Risk)
3. Third Level: Fraud Investigation Unit (Confirmed Fraud Indicators)
