ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) REGULATIONS

Document Version: 2.1
Effective Date: January 2026
Regulatory Authority: Financial Conduct Authority

TRANSACTION MONITORING REQUIREMENTS

1. SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY INDICATORS
   - Multiple small transactions totaling large amounts (structuring)
   - Rapid movement of funds across multiple accounts
   - Transactions with no clear business purpose
   - Involvement of shell companies or offshore entities
   - Cash-intensive businesses with unusual patterns

2. CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)
   Standard CDD Required:
   - Identity verification for all customers
   - Source of funds documentation
   - Regular account activity monitoring

   Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) Required:
   - High-net-worth individuals
   - Politically exposed persons (PEPs)
   - Businesses in high-risk sectors
   - Cross-border transactions over $5,000

3. REPORTING OBLIGATIONS
   - Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) must be filed within 30 days
   - Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) for cash over $10,000
   - Keep records for minimum 5 years
   - Cooperate with law enforcement investigations

4. RISK-BASED APPROACH
   Risk Level Assessment:
   - Low Risk: Domestic, established customers, regular patterns
   - Medium Risk: New customers, international transfers, cash transactions
   - High Risk: PEPs, high-risk jurisdictions, complex ownership structures

   Actions by Risk Level:
   - Low: Standard monitoring
   - Medium: Enhanced monitoring + periodic reviews
   - High: Continuous monitoring + senior management approval

5. COMPLIANCE PROCEDURES
   All financial institutions must:
   - Implement automated transaction monitoring systems
   - Train staff on AML indicators and procedures
   - Conduct annual AML compliance audits
   - Maintain board-level oversight of AML program
   - Report to regulatory authorities quarterly
